# Click Indicator + Showcase

For Geometry Dash 2.2074 / Geode 4.10.x.

## Features
- Cyan ring click indicator around Player 1.
- Pause menu button: Click ON/OFF.
- Pause menu button: Showcase.
- Showcase is a pre-programmed demo sequence and does not record the player.

The demo sequence is intentionally generic. It is not a universal bot for arbitrary levels; a level-specific showcase requires a sequence made for that level.
